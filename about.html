About TeqXus

TeqXus is a lightweight AI assistant and document reader designed to work when the internet is slow, expensive, or unavailable. We built it for people who need useful tools, not just another app that only works on fast Wi-Fi.

Why we built it

In many places across Africa and other regions with limited connectivity, people still need to ask questions, study, write documents, and stay productive. Most AI tools and document apps assume constant high-speed internet and large data plans. TeqXus takes the opposite approach.

We focused on three practical goals:

  • Low data usage — The interface is deliberately light so it loads and runs even on constrained connections.
  • Offline capability — Chat history and documents can stay on your device. You can continue working when the network drops.
  • Local languages and real needs — The assistant supports multiple languages and is tuned toward everyday questions people actually ask: health, farming, business, education, and daily life.

What TeqXus includes

TeqXus AI

A fast, mobile-friendly AI chat that works in several languages. It remembers conversation context on your device and can queue messages when you are offline, then send them when connectivity returns.

TeqDocs

A document reader and light editor for Markdown, Word (.docx), and PDF files. You can open files from your device or cloud storage, add bookmarks and highlights, and read with optional ambient sounds that help many people focus.

Who is behind TeqXus

TeqXus is developed by Teq Vault LLC. We are a small team focused on practical technology that works under real-world constraints. Our goal is not to build the flashiest demo — it is to build tools people can actually rely on day after day.

Our principles

  • Prefer local storage over unnecessary cloud dependency
  • Keep the interface simple and fast
  • Respect user privacy — most data never leaves the device
  • Design for low-bandwidth environments first
  • Be transparent about how the product works and how we handle data

Looking ahead

We continue to improve offline behaviour, language support, and document features. If you have feedback or ideas that would make TeqXus more useful in your community, we would like to hear from you.
